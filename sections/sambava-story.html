Madagascar • SAVA Region

Sambava, the origin of our Bourbon Vanilla

From Sambava, in northeastern Madagascar, Challenge Food connects the traditional knowledge of Malagasy vanilla producers with international buyers.

The journey of every vanilla bean

  1. Cultivation in the tropical environment of Madagascar.
  2. Manual pollination by skilled producers.
  3. Careful harvesting at maturity.
  4. Traditional curing, drying and preparation.
  5. Sorting, packing and export preparation.
