Professional vanilla extract enquiries

Vanilla extract is not a single universal specification. A professional enquiry should define the intended application, desired concentration, carrier or solvent requirements, certification requirements, packing format, quantity and destination market.

Information to include in your request

  • intended food or flavour application;
  • required concentration or fold if applicable;
  • carrier or solvent constraints;
  • organic, Fairtrade or other certification requirements;
  • packing size and total volume;
  • destination country and expected documentation.
Certification of raw vanilla beans does not automatically mean a processed vanilla extract is certified. The finished-product status must be confirmed specifically.
