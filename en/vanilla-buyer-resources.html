Why publish these resources?

International purchasing works better when both parties use the same vocabulary and document specifications clearly. These pages help buyers prepare precise enquiries, compare equivalent offers and identify information that should be confirmed before ordering.

These resources are informational and do not replace customs, regulatory or legal requirements applicable in the importing country.
