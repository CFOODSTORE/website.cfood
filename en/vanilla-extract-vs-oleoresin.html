Vanilla extract vs oleoresin: what is the difference?

Both are processed vanilla formats, but they are not interchangeable by default. Professional buyers should compare concentration, carrier or solvent, intended application, analytical specification, packing and certification status.

Vanilla extract

A vanilla extract is a liquid preparation that contains extracted vanilla flavour compounds in an agreed solvent or carrier system. The exact concentration, composition and regulatory definition depend on the requested product and market, so professional specifications must be confirmed before purchase.

Vanilla oleoresin

Vanilla oleoresin is a concentrated processed vanilla ingredient. Buyers typically need to define the desired concentration or fold, carrier or solvent, application, packing and analytical requirements. The term alone is not sufficient to describe a commercial specification.

Practical comparison for professional buyers

Extract

Often selected when a liquid vanilla preparation with a defined extraction system and concentration is required.

Oleoresin

Often selected where a more concentrated processed format is required and the technical specification is defined around fold, carrier and application.
