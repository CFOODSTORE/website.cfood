Madagascar Bourbon vanilla for import and distribution

Madagascar Bourbon vanilla, mainly produced from Vanilla planifolia, is widely used by professional buyers for premium food, flavour, extraction and retail applications. Challenge Food SARL prepares direct-origin offers based on the buyer's exact specifications rather than a fixed catalogue.

Grade A / Gourmet vanilla

Whole vanilla beans for pastry, gastronomy, premium retail and other high-value applications. Length, moisture and presentation are confirmed according to the available lot.

Grade B / Extraction vanilla

Vanilla beans for extraction, flavour manufacturing and industrial use. Specifications are prepared according to analytical needs, volume and packing.

A registered supplier based in Madagascar

Challenge Food SARL is registered in Madagascar under RCS Antananarivo 2022 B 00157. Its declared activities include import-export operations and the export of plant products, including vanilla. This supports a direct commercial relationship from origin and coordinated export documentation according to destination.

Specifications we can confirm before order

  • grade and proposed bean length;
  • target moisture or moisture range;
  • available volume and lot-specific MOQ;
  • professional packing and vacuum packing where appropriate;
  • lot documents, COA or specifications when available or required;
  • export and shipment documents applicable to destination.

Buyers can review our vanilla quality and document guide and our supplier verification checklist before placing an order.

Certified lots, extracts, essences, oleoresins and other special specifications are handled on request. Availability, certificate scope and technical documentation are confirmed in the quotation.

Looking for a Madagascar vanilla wholesaler or exporter?

For a useful quotation, include your required quantity, grade, bean length, moisture, packing, certifications if any, destination country and expected buying frequency. Industrial requests of 25 kg and above can also use our wholesale vanilla page.

Frequently asked questions

Does Challenge Food supply Grade A and Grade B vanilla?

Yes. Professional enquiries can cover Grade A / Gourmet and Grade B / Extraction vanilla, subject to the specifications and availability of the proposed lot.

What information is needed for a vanilla quotation?

Include the required grade, quantity, bean length, moisture, packing, destination country and any certification or analytical requirements.

Can Challenge Food provide quality documents?

Lot specifications, COA or other documents are confirmed according to the product, lot, destination and buyer requirement.

Do you handle industrial orders?

Yes. The dedicated industrial purchasing area is intended for requests from 25 kg. Other MOQs remain dependent on the specific product and lot.
