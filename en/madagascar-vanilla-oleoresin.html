What is vanilla oleoresin?

Vanilla oleoresin is a concentrated vanilla ingredient used in professional flavour and food applications. Unlike whole vanilla beans, it is a processed format, so the technical specification matters as much as the origin.

Information required for a quotation

  • requested concentration or fold where applicable;
  • carrier or solvent requirements;
  • intended food or flavour application;
  • required certifications;
  • packing format and total quantity;
  • destination country and required documentation.
A quotation for oleoresin must be based on an agreed technical specification. The exact composition, certification status and analytical documentation are confirmed per request.

Oleoresin, extract and whole vanilla beans

Different formats serve different uses. Whole Grade A beans are generally selected where presentation and whole-bean use matter; Grade B is often selected for extraction; extract and oleoresin are processed formats requiring their own specification.
