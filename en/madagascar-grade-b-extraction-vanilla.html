What is Grade B vanilla used for?

Grade B vanilla is commonly purchased for applications where processing performance is more important than premium whole-bean presentation. Typical buyers include extract manufacturers, flavour houses and food processors.

Extraction

Buyers may specify moisture, lot condition, analytical information, volume and packing according to their extraction process.

Industrial programmes

Recurring supply programmes may require agreed specifications, packing formats, documentation and purchasing frequency.

Why “Grade B” alone is not enough

The same commercial label can refer to different buyer expectations. We therefore request technical criteria before matching an available lot: intended use, target moisture if relevant, analytical needs, required volume, packaging and destination.

Lot and shipment documentation

  • offered-lot specification;
  • COA or analytical information when available or requested;
  • commercial invoice and packing list;
  • origin and phytosanitary documentation when applicable;
  • certification documents when an eligible certified lot is specifically offered.
Certification of raw vanilla beans does not automatically certify a finished extract or other processed product. Finished-product certification must be confirmed separately.

Grade B versus Grade A

Grade A Madagascar vanilla is generally preferred for gourmet, retail or applications where the whole bean is presented. Grade B is more commonly selected for extraction and processing. The better option depends on your process, quality targets and commercial application.

How to request an extraction lot

Tell us your intended use, quantity per order, expected frequency, destination, key analytical or quality parameters, preferred packing and any regulatory documentation required by your importer or quality department.
