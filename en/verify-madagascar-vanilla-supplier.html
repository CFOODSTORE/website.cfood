How to verify a Madagascar vanilla supplier

Challenge Food can provide available company and lot information on request for supplier qualification. For certified lots, supplier and/or producer verification can also be arranged according to the applicable documentation and certification scope.

1. Verify the seller’s legal identity

Request the exact legal name, address, registration references, professional contact details and contact person. Challenge Food SARL can provide available company information on request.

2. Request an offer linked to an actual lot

A useful offer should state grade, quantity, relevant characteristics, packing, destination and commercial terms discussed with the buyer.

3. Verify applicable documents

Quality documents are available on request according to the lot. Ask for the specification, COA when available or required, available analyses, export documents and certificates that genuinely apply.

4. Certified lot and producer verification

When a vanilla lot is certified, Challenge Food can arrange supplier and/or producer verification on request, subject to available records, lot traceability and the applicable certification scope.

5. Clarify payment, transport and export terms

Export terms are discussed according to destination and buyer requirements. Responsibilities, documents, transport and commercial terms should be confirmed before commitment.

6. Keep a written audit trail

Archive quotations, specifications, documents received, lot confirmations and commercial terms to maintain a clear documentary trail.

Producer verification applies to certified lots for which the relevant documentation and traceability are available. It is not a general guarantee applicable to every lot.
