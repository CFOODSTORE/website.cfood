From Madagascar origin to the destination market

A vanilla export operation combines lot qualification, agreed specifications, packing, commercial documentation and shipment coordination. The details depend on the destination market, product, volume and buyer requirements.

1. Specification

Grade, length, moisture, quantity, packing, certification and other requirements are defined before commitment.

2. Commercial offer

Pricing is prepared according to the actual lot, volume, destination and expected logistics scope.

3. Documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list, origin, phytosanitary and other applicable documents are coordinated for the shipment.

4. Shipment

Goods are prepared according to agreed packing and the logistics arrangement selected with the buyer.

Which documents may be required?

Requirements vary by importing country, importer, product and shipment method. Depending on the transaction, documentation may include:

  • commercial invoice;
  • packing list;
  • certificate of origin;
  • applicable phytosanitary documentation;
  • lot specification or COA when required;
  • certification documents when a specific eligible certified lot is offered.

The importer should verify its local requirements. Buyers should therefore identify destination-specific documents or analyses at the enquiry stage.

Grade A, Grade B and special requirements

For gourmet, premium retail and whole-bean use, see our Madagascar Grade A vanilla page. For extraction and processing, see Grade B extraction vanilla. Certified lots and processed vanilla products are handled case by case according to availability and documentation.

Import requirements differ by destination. The target market and importer requirements should be communicated before final confirmation of the export offer.

How to request an export quotation

Provide your company name, product, quantity, expected purchasing frequency, technical specifications, packing requirements, destination country or port and preferred Incoterm if known.
