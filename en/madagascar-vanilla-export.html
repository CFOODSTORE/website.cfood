From Madagascar origin to the destination market

An export transaction combines lot qualification, agreed specifications, packing, documentation and logistics coordination. Responsibilities, transport and commercial terms are defined with the buyer before final confirmation.

1. Specification

Grade, length, moisture, quantity, packing, certification and other requirements are defined before commitment.

2. Commercial offer

Pricing is prepared according to the actual lot, volume, destination and export terms discussed with the buyer.

3. Documentation

Applicable quality and shipment documents are available on request according to the lot and destination.

4. Shipment

Goods are prepared according to agreed packing and the logistics arrangement selected with the buyer.

Documents available on request

Depending on the lot and transaction, Challenge Food can provide applicable documentation on request, including commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary documents, lot specification, COA or certification documents when they genuinely apply to the offered lot.

Certified lots and verification

For certified vanilla lots, supplier and/or producer verification can be arranged on request, subject to available records and the certification scope applicable to the offered lot.

Regulatory and documentary requirements vary by destination. Export terms are therefore discussed and confirmed with the buyer before final commitment.

How to request an export quotation

Provide your company name, product, quantity, expected purchasing frequency, technical specifications, packing requirements, destination country or port and the export terms you prefer or wish to discuss.
