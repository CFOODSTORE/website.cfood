Vanilla powder for food and ingredient applications

Vanilla powder can be used where a dry-format ingredient is preferred. Professional buyers should confirm whether the requested product is pure ground vanilla, a specific granulometry, or a formulation with another carrier or ingredient.

Specifications to confirm

  • composition and ingredient declaration;
  • particle size or granulometry where required;
  • moisture and analytical parameters where applicable;
  • packing format and order quantity;
  • certification requirements;
  • destination and export documentation.
