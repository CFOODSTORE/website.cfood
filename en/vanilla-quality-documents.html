Quality documents on request

Depending on the lot and buyer specification, Challenge Food can provide available and applicable documents on request, including:

  • lot specification;
  • COA when available or required;
  • available analyses;
  • certification records that genuinely apply to the lot;
  • company documents used for supplier qualification;
  • applicable shipment documents.

Certified lots and traceability

For certified vanilla lots, supplier and/or producer verification can be arranged on request, subject to available records and the certification scope applicable to the offered lot.

Challenge Food does not present a certification, analysis or document as confirmed unless it applies to the relevant lot.

Shipment documents

Depending on destination and transaction, documentation may include commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary documents and other items required by the importer.

Professional packing

Packing is defined according to quantity, grade, transport duration and buyer requirements. Vacuum packing and export packing can be discussed during quotation.
