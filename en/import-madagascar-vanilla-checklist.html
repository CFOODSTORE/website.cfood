Madagascar vanilla import checklist

This checklist helps importers, wholesalers, distributors and manufacturers structure a first vanilla purchase from Madagascar, from supplier qualification through receiving inspection.

Before requesting a price

  • Define the use: retail, gastronomy, extraction, manufacturing or other.
  • State required grade and quantity.
  • Define length, moisture, packing and any analysis requirements.
  • Provide destination country and port.

Before selecting the supplier

  • Verify legal identity and professional contact details.
  • Request corporate documents required by your process.
  • Cross-check the supplier’s external presence where possible.
  • Clarify payment beneficiary and commercial terms.

Before confirming the lot

  • Receive a written specification for the offered lot.
  • Confirm grade, length range and expected moisture.
  • Request required analysis or COA where applicable.
  • Approve packing and labeling.
  • Use a sample or trial order when your process requires it.

Before shipment

  • Confirm Incoterm and each party’s responsibilities.
  • Check import documents required by your destination market.
  • Confirm invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and applicable phytosanitary paperwork.
  • Approve transport, possible insurance and delivery details.

At receiving

  • Check quantity and packaging integrity.
  • Compare received goods against the agreed specification.
  • Archive documents, photos and inspection results.
  • Document and report any non-conformity promptly.
Import requirements vary by country. This checklist is a general operational aid and does not replace customs, food-safety or legal rules applicable to your market.
