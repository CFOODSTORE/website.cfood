What does Grade A mean for vanilla buyers?

In professional vanilla trade, Grade A generally refers to whole beans suitable for gourmet or premium presentation. The label is not a single universal specification, so the practical criteria should be agreed for each lot. We therefore confirm bean length, target moisture, presentation, packing and any required documentation before commitment.

Typical uses

Pastry, gastronomy, premium retail, gifting, artisan products and applications where the full bean is visible or sold as a premium ingredient.

Buyer specifications

Requested length, moisture, quantity, packaging, certification requirements, destination and purchase frequency.

How we prepare a Grade A offer

A useful quotation starts with the buyer's real specification. We compare the requested characteristics with the available lot and confirm what can be supplied before order acceptance.

  • Madagascar origin and species information for the offered lot;
  • grade and length confirmed by lot;
  • professional packing, including vacuum packing when agreed;
  • lot specifications, COA or analysis when available or required;
  • export documents coordinated according to destination.

Grade A versus Grade B

Grade A is generally selected when presentation and whole-bean use are important. Grade B extraction vanilla is more commonly used by extractors, flavour houses and industrial processors. The right choice depends on the buyer's process and finished product.

We do not present one fixed moisture, length or analytical value as valid for every Grade A lot. Commercial specifications are confirmed against the actual lot offered.
