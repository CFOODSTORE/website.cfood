B2B Madagascar vanilla sourcing

A wholesale purchase should be based on a clear specification. Challenge Food prepares offers according to grade, quantity, bean length, moisture, intended use, packing and destination. This helps professional buyers compare commercially equivalent proposals.

Grade A / Gourmet

For premium retail, gastronomy, pastry and applications where whole-bean appearance matters.

Grade B / Extraction

For extraction, flavour manufacturing and industrial processing with specifications adapted to the buyer's process.

Trial lots and recurring supply

B2B requirements may range from a trial order to a recurring supply programme. MOQ, lead time, availability and price are confirmed according to the product and lot. For recurring demand, include expected purchasing frequency and forecast volume.

Professional packing

Packing can be discussed according to volume and destination, including vacuum packing, export packing, unit size or other specific requirements. Details are confirmed in the quotation before order.

Wholesale Madagascar vanilla pricing

A wholesale Madagascar vanilla price depends on grade, lot quality, volume, packing, documentation, destination and delivery terms. A quotation tied to an exact specification is therefore more useful than a generic price per kilogram.

International export

For overseas purchasing, see our page on Madagascar vanilla export. Applicable documents are coordinated according to the lot, shipping method and destination-market requirements.

Challenge Food does not present lot-dependent volumes, certifications or specifications as permanent guarantees. Each point is confirmed in the commercial offer.

What to include in a wholesale enquiry

  • company name and country;
  • required product and grade;
  • quantity or monthly/annual consumption;
  • length and moisture requirements if applicable;
  • packing format;
  • certification or analysis requirements;
  • destination and preferred Incoterm.

Wholesale vanilla FAQ

What is the industrial purchase threshold?

The dedicated industrial purchasing route starts at 25 kg. Product-specific MOQ, availability and commercial terms are confirmed in the quotation.

Can wholesale orders include different vanilla formats?

Professional enquiries can include whole beans and, depending on specification and availability, processed formats such as extract, powder or oleoresin.

Can packing and Incoterm be specified?

Yes. Packing and the preferred Incoterm should be included in the enquiry so the commercial offer can be prepared for the destination.
