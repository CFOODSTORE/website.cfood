A company based in Madagascar

Challenge Food SARL is registered with RCS Antananarivo under reference 2022 B 00157. Our commercial activity includes import-export operations and the export of plant products, including vanilla.

How we work with professional buyers

We focus on a clear supplier relationship: define lot specifications before commitment, document buyer requirements, discuss packing according to destination and coordinate applicable shipment paperwork.

Specification before quote

Grade, length, moisture, quantity, packing and destination are clarified to build a comparable offer.

Documents on request

Company documents, lot specifications, COA where applicable and shipment documents can be supplied according to need and availability.

B2B focus

The site is designed for importers, wholesalers, distributors, processors, flavour houses and manufacturers sourcing from Madagascar.

Direct contact

Commercial enquiries are centralized through commercial.export@cfood.store.

Verifiable external presence

Challenge Food already has several public third-party references. The company appears on Go Africa Online as an import-export business in Madagascar, on AlgoMtl with RCS reference 2022 B 00157, and in the “New list 2026” published by MadagascarVanilla.com.
