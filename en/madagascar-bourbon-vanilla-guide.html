Madagascar Bourbon vanilla: what a professional buyer should check

Vanilla quality is not defined by the word “gourmet” alone. Professional buyers should compare grade, bean length, moisture, appearance, intended use, traceability and lot documentation.

What is Madagascar Bourbon vanilla?

The commercial term “Bourbon vanilla” commonly refers to Vanilla planifolia grown in the Indian Ocean region, with Madagascar as a major origin. For professional sourcing, origin alone is not enough: each lot should be evaluated against clear specifications.

Grade A and Grade B: a practical distinction

Grade A is generally selected for premium retail, gastronomy and applications where appearance matters. Grade B is often purchased for extraction and industrial use. Commercial grade names can vary between sellers, so buyers should request lot-specific information covering bean length, moisture, condition and packing.

Moisture matters

Higher-moisture beans can be supple and visually attractive for certain uses, while drier beans may suit extraction or other storage and processing needs. The important point is to define the target range for the intended application and confirm it for the offered lot.

Bean length

Length affects presentation, sorting and sometimes price. Buyers should request a defined range or minimum bean length rather than relying only on broad quality terms.

Documents buyers may request

  • lot specification or technical sheet;
  • COA where available or required;
  • commercial invoice and packing list;
  • certificate of origin when applicable;
  • phytosanitary documents as required;
  • certification documents only when the specific lot is covered.
Certification for a raw vanilla lot does not automatically mean a processed extract or concentrate carries the same certification. The certification scope should be confirmed for the finished product.

Questions to ask before buying

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the lot, grade, packing and destination and should be confirmed in the commercial offer.

Can I request a sample?

Samples can be discussed according to destination, product type and shipping arrangements.

Can I request organic or Fairtrade vanilla?

Yes, subject to eligible lot availability and documentary verification.

How to request a comparable quotation

Provide quantity, grade, bean length, target moisture, packing, certification requirements, destination and expected buying frequency. This makes supplier quotations more transparent and comparable.
