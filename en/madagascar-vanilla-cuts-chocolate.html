Vanilla for chocolate: define the technical requirement

For chocolate use, the visual size of a whole bean is often less important than aroma, material condition, moisture, format and lot consistency. We therefore recommend a request based on your recipe and production process.

  • packing;
  • destination and document requirements.

Quality and traceability

Available quality documents and traceability information are provided on request according to the lot. For certified lots, supplier and/or producer verification may be arranged according to available records and certification scope.
